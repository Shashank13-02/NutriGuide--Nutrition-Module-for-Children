# Nutrition screening specification

The target population is birth through 72 completed months. Reviewed education
covers 0–59 months. Implemented numeric feeding indicators cover 6–23 months.
This is screening support, not clinical diagnostic software.

## Authoritative rule source

[WHO/UNICEF, Indicators for assessing infant and young child feeding practices (2021)](https://data.unicef.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Indicators-for-assessing-infant-and-young-child-feeding-practices.pdf),
defines MDD, MMF, MMFF and MAD and provides their calculation formulas. The implementation in
`intake_screening.py` is versioned `iycf-2021-v1`.

MDD counts at least 5 of 8 groups during the previous day. Infant formula counts
as Dairy; only human breast milk counts as Breast milk. MMF depends on age and
breastfeeding: 2 food feeds at 6–8m breastfed; 3 at 9–23m breastfed; 4 food/milk
feeds with at least one non-milk food feed at 6–23m non-breastfed. MMFF requires
2 fluid milk and/or semi-solid yogurt feeds. Semi-solid yogurt is included in
food feeds for MMF, so it is not added again to that count. MAD requires MDD and MMF, plus MMFF if non-breastfed.
Counts include meals and non-trivial snacks, across daytime and night-time.

These are population feeding-practice indicators. Applying them to a reported
record yields indicator statuses, not an individual validated severity score.
No arbitrary Low/Moderate/High category is computed.

## Input requirements and precedence

1. Validate exact input types, supported group/flag names and age (0–72 months).
2. Reported emergency symptoms yield emergency referral; other clinical concerns
   yield professional review. No model call or completed recall is required.
3. Without explicit confirmation, return insufficient data.
4. Outside 6–23 months, abstain from numeric IYCF classification.
5. Without a complete previous-day recall, abstain from indicator calculation.
6. Calculate known indicators, report missing fields, and do not compute MAD
   when required inputs are missing. Reject contradictory milk/food records.
7. Render indicator values, thresholds, findings, next steps, rule version and
   source references. Optional AI evidence selection cannot alter this object.

## Workflow outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| INDICATORS_MET | Required feeding indicators met in this recall; not proof of adequacy. |
| FEEDING_PATTERN_REVIEW | At least one required indicator not met or a reported food safety/choice concern. |
| INSUFFICIENT_DATA | Unconfirmed/incomplete/unsupported record; known partial indicators may be returned. |
| PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_FLAG | Reported symptom concern, with separate emergency flag and referral instruction. |

Legacy Low/Moderate/High names remain available only for compatibility with
externally supplied explanations. They are not outputs of this engine.

## Model boundaries

SLM role: select one reviewed evidence excerpt by ID, with JSON decoding constrained
to valid IDs or abstention; canonical output
is generated by deterministic rules. VLM role: candidate visible foods and
texture cues. Every draft requires caregiver review; no photographic calorie,
portion, deficiency or health prediction is performed. Vision food lists are validated
and converted into structured drafts; malformed responses abstain. Texture and
softness require caregiver review.
VLM person detection is not a validated privacy detector.

Vitamin D assessment, micronutrient prediction, therapeutic supplement advice,
clinical anthropometry and diagnosis are not implemented. Additional age bands
require separately reviewed evidence and independently labelled validation.

## Evidence and evaluation limits

Local model adaptation uses synthetic evidence-selection labels and held-out
question wording. Promotion tests output schema and exact selected evidence;
it is not clinical validation. The earlier benchmark contained simulated outputs
and fixed numeric scores; those claims have been removed. Existing evaluation
fixtures check scope/workflow language, not real-world diagnostic or VLM accuracy.
Country-level UNICEF aggregate spreadsheets are not individual clinical labels.
